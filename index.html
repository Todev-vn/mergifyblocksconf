Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Tap a column to drop your numbered block. When two blocks with the same value are adjacent (left, right, above, or below), they merge into a block with double the value. Chain merges cascade automatically — plan ahead for massive combos!

What is Pressure Drop?

In Normal difficulty and above, if you take too long to place a block (Think Time), the grid pushes up from below — adding pressure and reducing your available space. Place blocks quickly to avoid it!

How do I unlock Hard and Expert difficulty?

Hard mode unlocks when you reach a high score of 200,000. Expert mode unlocks at 500,000. Keep playing and improving to unlock them!

What do the power-ups do?

💣 Bomb — Tap any block on the grid to destroy it.
🔀 Swap — Tap two blocks to switch their positions.
⚡ Multiplier — Your next merge scores ×3 points.
↩️ Undo — Take back your last move.

How do I earn coins?

You earn coins by achieving high scores and through the Lucky Spin feature. Coins are used to purchase power-ups. Each difficulty level has its own cost multiplier.

Does the game require an internet connection?

No. The game is fully playable offline. Internet is only needed for Game Center leaderboards and watching rewarded ads for Lucky Spin.

Can I change the block style?

Yes! Open the in-game settings panel (gear icon ⚙️) to switch between available block skins during gameplay.

How do I reset my progress?

Go to Settings from the Main Menu, scroll down, and tap "Reset Data". This will reset your statistics, save data, achievements, and power-up counts. Your coins and personal settings will be kept.

What languages are supported?

The game supports English and Vietnamese (Tiếng Việt). The language switches automatically based on your device settings, or you can change it in the game settings.
